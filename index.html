// faq

Questions.

Will my images be uploaded to your servers? +
No. Images go directly from your machine to the Google API. Our servers only do license validation — one HTTPS request, then we're done. Your files stay yours.
How many machines can I activate? +
1 machine per license (HWID-bound). Switching computers? Email i@liangzai.org to request an unbind — usually handled within a few hours.
Does it work on macOS? +
v1.x is Windows only (10/11 64-bit). macOS support is planned for v2.0. Every purchase is a vote for prioritizing it — Founders' buyers get the upgrade at no extra charge within the v1.x line.
How does Gemini compare to Firefly for retouching? +
Faster (no Adobe middleware), cheaper (you use Google's free tier), and not tied to an Adobe subscription. Quality is Gemini 3.1 Flash Image — excellent for portraits, product shots, and scene extension. Extremely complex lighting scenarios may occasionally favor Firefly.
Why is it called Erect Banana? +
Gemini 3.1 Flash Image's internal codename is "Nano Banana2". This plugin makes Photoshop's AI fill capability stand up straight. That's the whole reason.
Do I need a paid Google account? +
No. Google AI Studio has a free tier at 15 requests/min — sufficient for most retouching sessions. If you process high volumes, you can upgrade your Google API plan directly.
