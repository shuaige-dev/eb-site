// FAQ

Questions.

常见问题。

Will my images be uploaded to your servers?我的图片会上传到你们服务器吗？ +
No. Images go directly from your machine to the Google API. Our servers only do license validation — one HTTPS request, then we're done. Your files stay yours.
不会。图片直接从你的电脑发到 Google API。我们的服务器只做 License 验证——一次 HTTPS 请求，之后再无通讯。文件永远是你的。
How many machines can I activate?一个 Key 能激活几台机器？ +
1 machine per license (HWID-bound). Switching computers? Email i@liangzai.org to request an unbind — usually handled within a few hours.
默认绑定 1 台机器（HWID 绑定）。换电脑了？发邮件到 i@liangzai.org 申请解绑，一般几小时内处理。
Does it work on macOS?支持 macOS 吗？ +
v1.x is Windows only (10/11 64-bit). macOS support is planned for v2.0. Every purchase votes for prioritizing it.
v1.x 仅支持 Windows 10/11 64位。macOS 版本计划在 v2.0 中实现。每一笔购买都是在为 Mac 版投票。
How does Gemini compare to Firefly?Gemini 和 Firefly 比怎么样？ +
Faster (no Adobe middleware), cheaper (free Google tier), not tied to an Adobe subscription. Quality is Gemini 3.1 Flash Image — excellent for portraits, products, and scene extension.
更快（没有 Adobe 中间层）、更便宜（谷歌免费额度）、不绑定 Adobe 订阅。模型是 Gemini 3.1 Flash Image，人物、产品图、场景扩展效果都很好。
Why is it called Erect Banana?为什么叫硬邦邦的香蕉？ +
Gemini 3.1 Flash Image's internal codename is "Nano Banana2". This plugin makes Photoshop's AI fill stand up straight. That's it.
Gemini 3.1 Flash Image 的内部代号是"Nano Banana2"。这个插件让 PS 的 AI 填充能力"硬"起来——仅此而已。
Do I need a paid Google account?需要付费的 Google 账号吗？ +
No. Google AI Studio has a free tier at 15 requests/min — sufficient for most retouching sessions. Upgrade your Google API plan if you need more.
不需要。Google AI Studio 有免费额度，15 次/分钟，普通修图完全够用。需要更多直接升级 Google 套餐即可。
